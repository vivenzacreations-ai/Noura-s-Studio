Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

Do you take bulk orders for weddings and events?
Yes, absolutely! We specialize in bulk orders for Biryani, Fried Rice, and authentic Kashmiri Wazwan. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance for large gatherings to ensure the highest quality and timely preparation.
Do you offer home delivery in Srinagar?
Yes, we offer home delivery in and around Botakadal, Gulshan Bagh, and nearby areas in Srinagar. You can easily place an order by clicking the "Order Now" buttons which will redirect you to WhatsApp.
What are your most popular dishes?
Our customers highly recommend our Chicken Tandoori, Special Chicken Biryani, Butter Chicken, and our Spicy Chowmein. For events, our authentic Wazwan is a crowd favorite.
